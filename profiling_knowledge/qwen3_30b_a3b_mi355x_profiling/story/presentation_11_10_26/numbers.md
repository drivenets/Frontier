## Definitions

Primary statistic: for every num_tokens present in BOTH datasets compute step_median/final_median, then take the median over tokens ('median_of_per_token_ratio'); min/max are over the same per-token ratios. 'ratio_of_medians' = median(step medians)/median(final medians) over the same tokens (alternative). 'max_abs_ratio_minus_1' = max |ratio-1|. Medians are time_stats.<op>.median. Ranges are on num_tokens.

Step → dataset: **original** = D1 (original, job 21313); **it1** = D2 (GC removed, job 21361); **it2** = D3 (GPU-bound (host fix), job 21356); **it3** = D4 (RoPE fix, job 21483); **final** = D5 (final, job 21539)
